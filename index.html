Poppy Goes to the Vet

Poppy is scared. Strange smells, strange animals, a cold table. Dr. Wanjiku is kind, but Poppy doesn't know that.

You can't explain it to her. But you can be there: stroke her, talk softly, give a treat at the right moment, and tell the vet when she's ready.

How to calm a puppy

  • Stroke her. Hold the button, and a hand strokes her slowly. Nothing calms her more. (You can also drag your finger gently over her. Wild rubbing winds her up.)
  • Talk softly. Hold the button and murmur to her. Stroking and talking together work best.
  • A treat takes her mind off things while she chews. Save it for the moment it's needed.
  • Read her. Licking her lips means she's a bit worried. Yawning, ears back: stressed. The whites of her eyes, tail tucked, trembling: very scared.
  • Go at her pace. Tell the vet to go ahead only when Poppy is calm. Rush her, and she gets more and more frightened, until the vet has to stop.

Stars show how calm she stayed, hearts how often you got the moment right.
